Vision 2045

From one studio to a creative group Indonesia can grow on.

In 2045 Indonesia turns 100. The young people starting school today will be running the country's companies by then. We want them to arrive with real work behind them, not just certificates.

  1. 2019

    The studio begins

    IbraCreative starts with typefaces and design work, and grows into a studio of 10+ designers.

  2. 2022

    Ibraz Academy opens

    Students join live client projects inside the studio and leave with a portfolio of shipped work.

  3. 2025

    Ibraz Group is formed

    The ventures come together under one holding, with Ibraz Capital investing the group's own capital.

  4. 2030

    A talent pipeline, city by city

    Academy cohorts beyond our home city, working with partner schools, and graduates hired into the studio and the industry.

  5. 2035

    Indonesian creative work, exported

    Fonts, animation and digital products made in Indonesia, sold to clients and audiences around the world.

  6. 2045 · Indonesia at 100

    A generation that learned by building

    Thousands of young Indonesians who did real design work early, now leading studios, brands and companies of their own.

Real briefs. Real clients. Real experience before graduation.
